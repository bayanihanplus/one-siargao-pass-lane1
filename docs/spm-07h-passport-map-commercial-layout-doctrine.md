# SPM-07H — Passport Map Commercial Layout Doctrine

## Hard Position

`/traveler/passport-map` is the Siargao Passport Map commercial app surface.

OSP remains the compliance/pass/QR/manifest/payment-clearance infrastructure underneath.

SPM exposes commercial journey discovery, Passport Trails, stamps, tours, retention, and AI Passport Assistant engagement.

## Approved Page Layout

1. Header
   - Siargao Passport Map
   - Notification

2. Hero / One Siargao Map
   - Journey map
   - progress
   - stamps
   - route state

3. Choose Your Passport Experience
   - Siargao Partner Tour
   - Passport Trails™ Curated Tour
   - Build Your Own Passport Trail

4. Featured Siargao Partner Tours
   - Actual local operator tour examples

5. Passport Trails™ Curated Tours
   - Official SPM trail families only

6. Build Your Own Passport Trail
   - DIY curated route builder

7. Guide Support / Provided By
   - Commercial support explanation only
   - No fake guide assignment

8. Your Trails
   - Traveler’s active / saved / stamped / pending progress

9. Verified Stops

10. Passport Assistant
    - SME prompt layer

11. Passport Rewards / Return Traveler Continuity
    - Gamification and retention preview

12. Events / Retention Preview

## Deleted Prime Section

The previous “How Passport Trails Work” instructional section must not occupy prime placement below the hero.

SPM should lead with commercial choice, not user documentation.

## Approved Product Categories

Use only:

1. Siargao Partner Tour
2. Passport Trails™ Curated Tour
3. Build Your Own Passport Trail

## Official SPM Trail Families

Use only:

1. Island Hopping Trail
2. Surf Explorer Trail
3. North Siargao Trail

5. Culture & Community Trail
6. Sunset & Scenic Stops Trail
7. Adventure Trail
8. Return Traveler Continuity Trail

## Guide Support Logic

Siargao Partner Tour:
- Guide/support provided by the approved local partner.

Passport Trails™ Curated Tour:
- Guide/support provided through SPM-approved trail operations.

Build Your Own Passport Trail:
- Route guidance provided by SPM.
- Approved local partner support may apply where transport, boat, guide, or activity service is required.

## Commercial Pricing Logic

Use implicit commercial readiness messaging only on the SPM landing page.

Allowed:
- Package pricing applies when booking opens.
- Pricing appears before checkout.
- Request or booking activation depends on readiness.
- Planning preview now.
- Partner fulfillment may apply where service is required.

Not allowed:
- fake price
- fake checkout
- fake booking
- fake operator approval
- fake guide assignment
- fake stamp completion

## AI Assistant Role

The AI Passport Assistant is the SME layer for:
- choosing Passport Experience
- explaining tour types
- explaining Passport Stamps
- explaining QR verification
- explaining guide/support logic
- helping plan DIY trails
- retention and next unlock guidance

It must not confirm booking, payment, guide assignment, manifest approval, or stamp completion unless backend state proves it.
