# SPM-R1 CONFIRMED REFINEMENT ADDENDUM

## Confirmed Additions

The following geography/tourism areas must be included in the Siargao Geography + Tour Sites Research Doctrine and must be carried forward into SPM-R2 Node Classification Matrix:

1. Socorro Island / Bucas Grande
2. Giwan, Union, and Secret Spot
3. Little Hawaii

These are included as research and classification subjects.

They are not automatically approved for public activation, stamp issuance, checkout, or operator fulfillment until they pass SPM-R2 readiness gates.

---

## 1. Socorro Island / Bucas Grande

### Correct Classification

Socorro / Bucas Grande must be included as a major adventure and marine-tourism geography cluster.

Doctrine wording must remain careful:

- Socorro is the municipality context.
- Bucas Grande is the island/island-group tourism context.
- Sohoton Cove, Jellyfish Sanctuary, caves, lagoons, and related water routes belong under the Socorro / Bucas Grande adventure cluster.
- This cluster must not be treated as ordinary General Luna island hopping.
- This cluster must not be modeled as a casual DIY public QR trail.

### Trail Family Mapping

Primary:
- Adventure Trail

Secondary / related:
- Island Hopping Trail only where a specific package route legally and operationally connects island movement
- Return Traveler Continuity only through verified completion history

### Candidate / Official Node Subjects for SPM-R2

- SOCORRO_BUCAS_GRANDE_CLUSTER
- SOHOTON_COVE
- JELLYFISH_SANCTUARY
- HAGUKAN_CAVE
- MAGKUKUOB_CAVE
- TIKTIKAN_LAKE_OR_COVE
- BUCAS_GRANDE_ADVENTURE_ROUTE
- SOHOTON_OPERATOR_JUMP_OFF
- APPROVED_SOCORRO_BOAT_CHECKPOINT

### Safety / Compliance Flags

Required flags:

- boat_required
- vessel_required
- manifest_relevant
- operator_required
- guide_required
- safety_controlled
- environmental_sensitivity
- weather_dependent
- QR_validation_required
- stamp_only_after_validation
- no_DiY_only_completion
- no_instant_checkout_until_operator_pricing_and_availability_are_confirmed

### Hard Rule

Socorro / Bucas Grande must be treated as a controlled Adventure and marine-movement cluster.

It must not be reduced into:
- generic island hopping
- public scenic stop
- self-guided stamp route
- shallow tour-card listing

## 2. Giwan, Union, and Secret Spot

### Correct Classification

Giwan, Union, and Secret Spot must be included under Surf Explorer research.

These are surf-area candidates and must not be confused with non-Siargao locations.

### Trail Family Mapping

Primary:
- Surf Explorer Trail

Secondary:
- North / coastal discovery only if route geography supports it
- Return Traveler Continuity only through verified surf participation or repeat-trip progression

### Candidate Node Subjects for SPM-R2

- GIWAN_SURF_AREA
- UNION_SURF_AREA
- SECRET_SPOT_SURF_AREA

### Readiness Status

Default readiness:
- CANDIDATE / PENDING_REVIEW

Reason:
These surf spots require local validation for:
- exact local naming
- access points
- safety suitability
- beginner/intermediate/advanced classification
- surf school/operator relationship
- whether QR stamp should represent visit, session, or coach-confirmed participation

### Surf Validation Logic

Possible validation types:

1. Site Visit QR
   - For public surf-viewing / surf-culture discovery
   - No surf participation claim

2. Surf School / Instructor Confirmation
   - For lesson or surf session
   - Required before participation stamp

3. Operator / Guide Confirmation
   - For guided surf route or multi-stop surf discovery

### Safety / Commercial Flags

Required flags:

- surf_condition_dependent
- weather_dependent
- skill_level_sensitive
- operator_required_if_lesson
- surf_school_required_if_session
- no_advanced_completion_without_validated_session
- QR_validation_required
- stamp_only_after_validation

### Hard Rule

Giwan, Union, and Secret Spot may be included in the research registry, but they must not be activated as official completion nodes until local surf validation is completed.

## 3. Little Hawaii

### Correct Classification

Little Hawaii must be included as a North Siargao / Scenic candidate node.

It may also be referenced with related naming such as:
- Hawaiian Hills
- Little Hawaii Viewpoint
- Secret Mountain Viewpoint where locally validated

### Trail Family Mapping

Primary:
- North Siargao Trail
- Sunset & Scenic Stops Trail

Secondary:
- Return Traveler Continuity only through verified repeat-trip or route completion

### Candidate Node Subjects for SPM-R2

- LITTLE_HAWAII
- HAWAIIAN_HILLS
- SECRET_MOUNTAIN_VIEWPOINT

These may later resolve into one canonical node or multiple separate nodes depending on local validation.

### Readiness Status

Default readiness:
- CANDIDATE / PENDING_REVIEW

Reason:
Little Hawaii / Hawaiian Hills requires validation for:
- exact name
- access path
- municipality / barangay
- public vs private access
- safety / road condition
- whether it can support QR validation
- whether it should be scenic-only or guided-route attached

### Safety / Access Flags

Required flags:

- exact_location_unverified
- public_access_unverified
- road_condition_check_required
- safety_review_required
- QR_validation_required_if_activated
- no_instant_checkout
- no_public_activation_until_access_is_confirmed

### Hard Rule

Little Hawaii must not be seeded as an approved official scenic node until access, naming, and safety conditions are verified.

It should enter SPM-R2 as a candidate scenic node.
